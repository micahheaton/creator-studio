METHOD   /   FIELD NOTES

Keep the cause and the effect.

The pad is on the left of the widescreen demo. Its signal lands on the scope on the right. Slice the middle out and the cable gets a lovely portrait. The demonstration disappears.

Try the crop math

A 1920 × 1080 source yields a central slice only 607.5 pixels wide for a 9:16 crop. Anything left of about x = 656 or right of x = 1264 falls outside it. That is enough room for a cable and remarkably little context.

Give each object a job

Write two nouns and one verb before choosing a frame. Here the pad triggers the scope. Put them on opposite ends of the wide frame, side by side in square, then stack them in vertical. The 4:5 feed version needs a shorter stack. Watch where the labels land after moving either object.

Choose a caption band

Try the upper band if a platform puts controls over the lower one. Keep the caption away from the active pad and signal trace. Open the actual export at phone size, with the destination controls visible, before publishing.

What this tool can move

The fixture contains two independent drawing layers and two fixed caption choices. The focal controls position those layers within the authored composition. A flattened recording with both subjects baked in needs separate source layers or a new edit.
