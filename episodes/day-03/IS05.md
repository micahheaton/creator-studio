# The middle looks fine. The tail does not.

**IS05 · Day 3 · IntegratedSOC**

A median can look healthy while a single case-relevant event is late.

**Method:** Compare event and arrival timestamps on the same clock.

**Sample result:** Median arrival: 2 minutes · One source arrived at 8 min · Threshold breached.

**Decision:** Investigate the slow source before interpreting a quiet dashboard.
